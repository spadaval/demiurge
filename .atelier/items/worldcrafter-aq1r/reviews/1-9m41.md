## Finding 1: Prevent section rebuilds from sending or restoring the previous request
Recommendation: fix now

`app/src/app/generation.ts:352-356` saves the new selection and awaits `openRequest`, but leaves the old slip fully sendable while the bible and request are rebuilt. `openRequest` then unconditionally publishes its result at lines 329-337, without checking that this is still the current slip or latest selection. The viewer also leaves the section buttons enabled during that interval (`app/src/generate/context-viewer.tsx:141`), so another change can be derived from the previous breakdown.

I reproduced this with the real `createGeneration` service on the lighthouse fixture. After opening the arrival request, I held `storyRecords.list()` pending and called `selectSections({ pinned: [], excluded: ["People in it"] })`. The slip still referenced the original request. Calling `sendSlip(null)` sent a body containing `## People in it`. I then closed the slip and released the read; the pending section rebuild reopened it. These are consequences of an ordinary delayed IndexedDB read, with no sleeps or failed storage operations required.

Track section reassembly as part of the slip's state so Send cannot send the previous request after a section action. Ensure completion belongs to the current slip and latest selection before publishing it; closing or replacing the slip must invalidate the pending result. Prevent or correctly combine further section actions while rebuilding. Add a gated-read regression that checks sending during a change and closing/replacing the slip before the change completes.
