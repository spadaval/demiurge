## Goal

Fix what the panel in worldcrafter-efis judges before anyone reads a run, so the judges can't shape the rubric to what they find.

## Done when

`docs/verdicts/long-run-rubric.md` is committed and independently reviewed before any judge reads a run. It asks, for each finished story, with citations to the story or the notes:
- where the story falls short as a finished story: dropped threads, setups with no payoff, contradictions, things the reader learns too early or too late, motivations that drift, scenes that don't turn, and anything else;
- how much each shortfall costs the story;
- whether the notes held what the drafting sessions needed, or whether sessions lost or contradicted what had been decided.

It also says how each judge ranks the shortfalls, and how the shortfalls named by at least two judges are combined into one ranked list. The panel follows the "Reading one arm" case in the Verdicts section of `docs/strategies/notes-first.md`.
