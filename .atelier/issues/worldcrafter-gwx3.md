## Outcome

A fresh writer agent, using Demiurge (the story CLI and skill), builds a rich world and a novel-scale outline on the warlock-and-"Chat" prompt, then writes four scenes at points the orchestrator picks. A four-judge panel scores each scene, read alone with its story so far, against a fixed rubric whose thesis criterion is world aliveness. Round 0 fixes the tool first; up to five rounds follow, each fixing the tool and skill from what the writer's transcript and model show. The round log (tool changes, how the writer used the tool, every judge's scores with quotes) is the evidence. Scores are per judge and indicative only (decision worldcrafter-jeue, strategy docs/strategies/world-demo.md).

## Success criteria

- Round 0's six fixes (not counting its docs housekeeping task) are each independently reviewed and merged locally.
- The rubric is written and reviewed before any judge reads anything.
- Each round's four scenes, story-so-far texts, point choices, judge reports and round log are recorded.
- The mission ends after five rounds or after a round with no meaningful tool or skill issues.
