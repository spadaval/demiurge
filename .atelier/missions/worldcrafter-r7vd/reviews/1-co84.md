## Finding 3: lgz8 requires the manuscript view to follow story order while UI is out of scope
Recommendation: fix now

lgz8 Done when: "scene ordering in the story bible drives the manuscript view without duplicating outline state" (`.atelier/items/worldcrafter-lgz8/item.md` line 27). Mission and lgz8 Scope put UI views in `worldcrafter-mtkn`.

Either lgz8 is a data-layer item (add a scene-to-document link and a reading-order query; leave `createAppManuscript` to later UI work) or it is allowed to change `manuscript.ts` and that line is in scope. As written, an implementer who rewires the rail is out of scope, and one who does not cannot tick the Done when line.

Also say what happens to stored outline books and chapters. Structure-first treated chapters as packaging; the rebuild stored them. Dropping `outlines` without a mapping loses chapter titles the current app shows.

## Finding 4: lgz8's collection list does not match the story graph or the time store
Recommendation: fix now

lgz8 lists actors, drives, events, causes, facts, objects, counts, memberships, versions, knowledge, open questions, reader questions, scenes, deliberate gaps. `Story` in `app/src/story/story.ts` also has `premise`, `participants` (`Participant` is `{ event, who, role }`), and an embedded `timeline: Timeline` (events, positions, frames, constraints). Commands include `setParticipant` / `removeParticipant` (`commands.ts` lines 297–301). A store that omits participants cannot round-trip Round 5 `story.json`.

Events are listed as a story collection while the mission keeps `app/src/time/` modular and the adapter already has `timelines`. `createTimelineStore` persists one `StoredTimeline` at key `"world"` and answers S10 queries from that plus the outline (`app/src/time/store.ts`). Story commands already apply `TimelineCommand`s to `story.timeline`. Persisting events twice, or only in story collections, will desync S10 from the bible.

Say that commands still run on an assembled `Story` (as `commands.ts` does today) and name the persistence boundary: one story blob, normalized collections, and which of `timelines` or story `events` is source of truth. Include participants and premise. Core primitive changes (a `documentId` on `Scene`, splitting `timeline`) are Authority and need to be written down for Sumeet.

## Finding 5: replacing `world/` is unspecified for kinds that have no story primitive
Recommendation: fix now

The outcome is one bible "replacing the split with `app/src/world/` and `app/src/outline/`" (`mission.md` line 27). World entries are per-document fact sidecars with kinds `note`, `scene`, `person`, `place`, `organization`, `culture`, `magic_system`, `technology`, `natural_law`, `species` (`app/src/world/kinds.ts`). Story actors are only `person` | `organization`. World facts are time-indexed field changes with asserted/inferred authority (`app/src/world/facts.ts`). Story facts are `{ id, text, about }`.

There is no mapping. Cleanup of "overlapping wiring" can delete place and culture entries, or leave two fact systems in the same IndexedDB. Existing generation (`app/src/context/`, `app/src/app/generation.ts`) still reads `WorldStore`. 3z9j is supposed to feed the unified bible into the Context Viewer, so lgz8 has to say whether those documents remain documents, become story facts/objects, or wait.

Write the keep/map/drop rule for each world kind and for `worldEntries`, and whether generation keeps using `WorldStore` until 3z9j.
