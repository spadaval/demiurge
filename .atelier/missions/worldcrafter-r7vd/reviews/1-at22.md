## Finding 1: The plan doesn't say which model wins where `app/src/story/` and `app/src/world/` + `app/src/time/` disagree, and that is a core-primitive call
Recommendation: fix now

The two models are not the same records stored in two places. They disagree on primitives:

- Scenes. In `app/src/story/story.ts:10-15` a scene is a plain record with no place in story time; `depicts` is its only link to events. In `app/src/time/timeline.ts:11-14` every outline scene *is* an event (`sceneEventId(documentId)`) and exists while it is in the outline.
- Actors. Decision worldcrafter-ddha (`docs/rebuild/story-model.md:130-136`) says actors stay entry documents in the app, and that the headless file's plain-record actors and scenes, and facts that don't change at events, are a simplification a keep verdict "has to map one onto the other".
- Facts. `app/src/world/facts.ts` holds time-indexed facts on entries; `app/src/story/` facts don't change at events.

`worldcrafter-lgz8` says to persist "the battle-tested story bible (`app/src/story/`)" and replace `world/` and `outline/`, which silently picks the headless file's side on all three. The mission's Authority says changes to core story primitives come back to Sumeet, and it says nothing about this mapping. The mission also cites "Sumeet's direction on 2026-11-22" with no decision ID, while decision worldcrafter-2y4g and `story-model.md:126` still say the story lives in one readable file git can diff.

Fix: record the 2026-11-22 direction as a decision, and either state the mapping in the mission (scene as record vs event, actor as record vs entry document, facts with or without time) with Sumeet's yes, or add a needs-human decision item that blocks `worldcrafter-lgz8`.

## Finding 2: The queries the tool registry wraps are in `app/scripts/story.ts`, not `app/src/story/commands.ts`, and they read prose files from disk
Recommendation: fix now

The success criterion and `worldcrafter-7r87` say every mutation and query "in `app/src/story/commands.ts`" becomes a tool. `commands.ts` holds only the mutations (`StoryCommand`, `applyStoryCommand` at line 337). The reads `show`, `reader-order` (with `--before`), `span`, `trace`, `touches`, `gaps` and `contradictions` are assembled in the 3721-line CLI `app/scripts/story.ts` (`READS` at line 1783, `showRecord` at 2026, `readerOrderLines` at 2540, `spanLine` at 2621). That file imports `node:fs`, `node:os` and `node:path`, and `trace` reads each scene's prose from a markdown file beside the story (`proseFile` at 2013, `readFile` at 2890, line-numbered targets at 2907-2908).

So `worldcrafter-7r87` is really "move the read layer out of the CLI into `app/src/story/` as structured, fs-free functions, and make `trace` read prose from the scene's Plate document", and the CLI then calls those. `worldcrafter-667u` depends on the same work for its `reader-order --before` context packet. As written, an implementer reading "commands.ts" will underestimate the item by most of its size, and the "no shell, no filesystem" line has no plan behind it.

Fix: correct the success criterion and `worldcrafter-7r87`'s Goal and Done when to name the CLI read layer and the port of `trace`'s prose source, and say the CLI keeps working by calling the moved functions.

## Finding 3: Retiring `app/src/world/` and `app/src/outline/` breaks `app/src/time/` and `app/src/context/`, and `worldcrafter-lgz8` holds both the new store and the retirement
Recommendation: fix now

The mission's Out list says `time/`'s frame math stays modular, but `time/` is built on the stores the plan removes, and mission worldcrafter-3otd's S4 (generation with world cards) and S10 (story time under frames) are shown by code on that side.

`worldcrafter-lgz8` puts all of this in one item: new storage collections, scene-to-document links, a manuscript view driven by bible order, `story.json` import and export, and the "migration/cleanup" of `world/` and `outline/`. Give (b) a Done when that can be checked, such as no imports of `world/` or `outline/` left in `app/src/`.

## Finding 7: The plan doesn't carry mission 3otd's sync-ready rules into the new collections
Recommendation: defer

`applyStoryCommand` is pure over a whole `Story`, and `Story.scenes` is "in telling order" (`story.ts:404-405`). Mission worldcrafter-3otd's S9 requires every stored record to have a client ID and an `updatedAt`, and deletes to leave tombstones (`worldcrafter-4t98`). Storing the bible as one record per world is simplest but syncs coarsely. Storing one record per collection item needs an explicit order for scenes and an `updatedAt` that story records don't have. The layout is the implementer's call under the Authority section, but `worldcrafter-lgz8` should say S9 applies so the choice is made with it in view.

## Finding 8: The prose profile can't assume tool calling on finetunes
Recommendation: defer

`worldcrafter-667u` binds a finetune or local model for prose drafting. Many featherless finetunes and local GGUFs have chat templates with no tool support, and `llama.cpp` serves tools only with `--jinja` and a template that has them. The runner should treat tools as optional per profile and degrade to a plain completion, and `worldcrafter-erje`'s tests should cover a backend that rejects or ignores `tools`. That can go in the items when they start.
