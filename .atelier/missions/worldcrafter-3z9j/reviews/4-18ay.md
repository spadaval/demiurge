## Finding 2: Snapshot retention could thin the sync-conflict snapshots that `ji5d` relies on
Recommendation: defer
`ji5d` (line 32) promises that a losing local draft is kept as a device-local snapshot "so neither device's prose is ever silently lost". `worldcrafter-my9t` (lines 20-24) protects only the newest, AI-accept and repair snapshots from thinning. A conflict snapshot is usually not the newest one once the writer keeps editing, so a thinning rule written to `my9t` as it stands could delete it. Whichever of the two lands second should give conflict snapshots their own reason and add them to the protected set, with a test. Deferred because neither item changes this round and no thinning ships before Sumeet approves `my9t`.

## Finding 3: Non-document records drop the losing local edit with no copy
Recommendation: no action
`ji5d` (line 33) says that for bible records, annotations and requests "the server's newer revision wins cleanly". If dogfooding shows lost hand edits (more likely once `worldcrafter-o7to`'s direct controls ship), a conflict log or notice is the follow-up.
