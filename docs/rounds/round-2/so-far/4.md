Bry is a warlock with no single patron. Instead, hundreds of lesser powers watch her through a scroll that runs up her left eye, and she calls them Chat. They vote on her spells, pay her in sparks of every colour and argue among themselves. Gorehound always wants a fight. Lantern gives directions. Thimble, a small flame, hushes the lewd ones. Mothlight, pale silver, keeps asking whether she has eaten. In Lessering she hunts ghouls under the tannery, sings at the Leaning Gull for Dov's stew, and writes each new watcher's name in her guestbook.

Then Corvin arrives with a writ sealed in green wax stamped with a hawthorn. He is a warlock whose Queen has taken his name, and Mothlight falls silent whenever he is near. Bry runs. Chat votes to fight, and she knocks him into the river. Ferro's Pactwardens raid the Gull, arrest Dov and tear the licence off the door. Corvin finds Bry and offers a way out: plead at the Long Night Court, win a licence, and Dov goes free. Bry agrees. She doesn't tell him why: her mother's note, left behind when she vanished, bore the same hawthorn seal.

On the road north, Bry saves a toll bridge with help from a faint new voice in the scroll, Quillon, the forgotten god of tolls. His last priest, Hal, joins them because the Court will need someone who reads old law. When Bry's watcher count sags, the candle-faced Mr. Tallow gives her a brass coin. The next morning hundreds of new watchers arrive, all calling themselves "just a friend". They never disagree, and their brass sparks make her stronger than she has ever been.

At the Drowned Mill her rival Lark Sabine takes Bry's own advice, "give them what they want," and walks out to the Grindle. The brass bloc outvotes Bry's regulars, she burns instead of pulling, and Lark drowns. Lark's watchers come over to Bry. Afterwards Bry tells Corvin she has never once closed her window, and he tells her about the brother he watches but no longer visits. Thimble warns her that the friends are one thing wearing many faces. Bry calls it jealousy, and that night Thimble disappears.

In the Sallow Marsh, Bry refuses a brass vote for the first time and nearly loses a fight with Ferro. Then silent Grandmother Cinder helps her, and Ferro lets them go. Hal finds the Lion's Share in old law: a power holding more than half her sparks before the Court holds all of her. Mothlight then reveals that the crow-man took the singer of Lessering into the Hill. Corvin admits he took Bry's mother to sing for the Queen, so the Queen would spare Bry's voice. Bry sends him away.

Tallow offers to buy her mother back. Hal reads the small print aloud and recognises the seal of the Brass Marquis. Bry and Hal raid the Marquis's counting-house, and Quillon spends nearly everything he has to free Thimble. Thimble reports that the Marquis will claim Bry at the solstice.

In a snowbound shepherd's hut, Corvin returns. He has turned on his Queen. He tells Bry the cage is empty: her mother flew through a mirror as something pale, with wings. Remembering Mothlight, Bry says nothing. A single silver spark lands on her hand, and she holds it all night.
