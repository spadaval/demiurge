Bry is a warlock without a single patron. Hundreds of small ones watch her all at once, and she calls them Chat. Their messages scroll up her left eye. Thimble keeps out the worst of them. Gorehound always wants a fight. Lantern gives directions, usually right. Mothlight, pale silver, asks whether she has eaten. Chat votes on her spells and pays her in sparks of every colour, and she carries a mirror shard on a cord. Under Vossick's tannery in Lessering they voted frost over fire, and she froze a ghoul nest. At the Leaning Gull she sang for Dov's stew while a sour Lark Sabine asked how she did it. At night she wrote new names in her guestbook and slept with the window open, as she always does.

Then Corvin arrived with a writ under a green hawthorn seal. Chat named him Crowboy, and Mothlight went silent the moment he walked in. The seal chilled Bry for reasons she couldn't name, and she ran. In Ropewalk Alley he outfought her but took care not to hurt her, and she knocked him into the river. Ferro's Pactwardens raided the Gull, took Dov away and tore the licence off the door. Corvin found Bry on the roof over the garret where her mother left her. The only way to free Dov, he said, was to win a licence at the Long Night Court. Her mother's note carried the same hawthorn seal. She agreed and didn't tell him why.

Corvin's Queen took his name, and he hears her only twice a year. Bry has never had a silent minute. At Hob's Crossing she held a collapsing bridge while a faint voice called Quillon told her how to set the keystone. Hal, its keeper, knew the name: Quillon was his god, who had never left, only grown too small to be heard. Hal, who reads old law, joined them. Six days of rain sent her count falling, and in Crake's market the candle-faced Mr. Tallow gave her a brass coin stamped with scales. Corvin warned against unearned gifts. She took it anyway. Overnight came hundreds of watchers who all called themselves "just a friend". They never disagreed, and they made her stronger than she had ever been.

At Fennick's Ford, Lark was performing for the voices Thimble had shut out of Bry's chat. Bry told her to give them what they wanted. What they wanted was the Grindle at the Drowned Mill. Lark sang to it and was taken. The brass outvoted Bry's call to PULL her out and made her BURN instead, and Corvin brought Lark up drowned. Later Bry told him about the silent garret and the night Thimble first answered. He told her about his brother Tobin. Thimble warned her the friends were a single thing. Bry called Thimble jealous, and Tallow's light stole Thimble away while she slept.

With Thimble gone, Chat ran wild. In the Sallow Marsh Bry refused a brass vote for the first time. Grandmother Cinder, who never speaks, helped her frost hold, and Ferro let them go. Corvin heard Bry sing her mother's song. Hal found the Lion's Share: whoever holds half her sparks before the Court holds all of her. Mothlight revealed that the crow-man had taken the singer of Lessering into the Hill. Corvin admitted it. Her mother had gone willingly to save Bry's voice. Chat voted KILL HIM, Hal stood in the way, and Bry sent Corvin away. Under the Hill he found the cage empty. Her mother was gone, and the Queen wanted Bry in her place, so he resolved to betray his patron. Quillon caught the Brass Marquis's clause in Tallow's contract. At Grey Ford, Quillon spent nearly everything to free Thimble. Now Hal lies feverish in a shepherd's hut. The Marquis will make his claim at midnight on the solstice. Hal has asked what happens to a pact if the window closes. Bry sits up all night with it open.
