Brannoc, a man of oath and prayer, was hired to clear the Marle Bridge cistern. He found a young woman, Kit, already there, haggling with the dark over the price of a firebolt. Her spells flared as if someone were paying for them. Together they killed the ghoul. Kit is a warlock with no single patron but hundreds of lesser ones, all watching her at once, and she calls them Chat.

At the Drowned Lantern Kit explained Chat as a comic routine, reading from the scroll where their voices appear: Silt, Gristle, who wants blood, and Pennywhistle, a joker. There are rules: the Window, tithes, requests, and the Wardens who police such pacts. Brannoc told her of his god Iver's silence and a last vision. She mocked it and said she was nobody's lantern.

Counting her gallery at dawn, Kit found more names but fewer voices. Dozens of grey names watch and never tithe or speak. Lampwick, a quiet patron, tithes a single mote whenever she does something she doesn't perform. When Ilse Marrable's wardens cornered Kit over her unlicensed pact, Brannoc claimed the old right of the walker in the dark, and Ilse honoured it. She warned Kit that Hollin the Many-Eyed is why the Concordance exists.

Ysolde, a scholar who keeps banned books, explained the law of majority holding. Kit counted seventy-one grey names out of three hundred and twelve: someone is buying her patrons. The buyer is Corvin Thale, a courteous, tired factor of the House of Pale Coin. He serves the Exchequer and keeps a ledger that writes back about a Reckoning and his surety. A grey name offered Pennywhistle the Exchequer's terms, sustenance for ever and no fading. Pennywhistle told no one.

Kit tried to grow faster than the buyer could buy. She staged a public fight with the Weir Wyrm and took Chat's payment for a grander finish. The weir-gate burst, and young Tobin was swept away. Brannoc saved him, though Tobin's arm was broken. Corvin then called on Kit and told her that Maud is alive and indentured in the Counting House. If Kit sells the pact, Maud goes free and Kit gets one silent patron. He left a pale coin to spend at a midnight crossroads. Kit threw him out, then asked Chat to be quiet. They weren't.

Kit, Brannoc and Ysolde set off by barge toward Saltcombe and Hollin's testament. At Orrin Bridge the grey names withheld their power together, and Kit's spell died. Brannoc went into the river in his mail, and Kit pulled him out without magic. While he lay feverish, she told him how her pact began, alone for nine days in the Sump. When he asked why she had been alone, she changed the subject.

Brannoc caught Ysolde sending a letter by crow. She admitted Corvin is her former student, who took the Exchequer's pact to pay her debts. Kit kept her on anyway. Upstream, Corvin burned the letter and bought the fading Pennywhistle, who begged him not to tell Kit. His ledger now reads 196.
