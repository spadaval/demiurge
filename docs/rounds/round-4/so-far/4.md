Tilly is a warlock in the city of Sallowcross with no single patron. Instead, hundreds of small gods watch through her eyes at once and throw her power. She calls them Chat, after the stonechats on the Foundling House wall. Among them are Mother Sluice, the old canal goddess; pedantic Old Hob; Bellwether, who rings for a melted bell; Gnash, who heckles everything; quiet Thimble; tiny Pip; and Greyhand, who never spoke and threw gold. Tilly lives at the Kettle Street Marionette Theater with Rook, a drunk showman. When she says "Curtain", Chat goes dark so she can sleep.

Casimir, a Warden, caught her coming out of the drowned cellars. He was tracking whoever was "hollowing" people, leaving them alive with white, blank eyes. Wardens suspected small gods. His own patron, the Abbess of Hours, has never spoken to him. When Wick, a boy from the theater, was hollowed, Tilly made a deal with Casimir to hunt the hollower together. At that moment a silent new patron joined Chat, marked with nine small bells.

Their search led to the Sump, the flooded quarry where small gods were thrown to starve during the Tidying two hundred years ago. Tilly made her pact there. A feral gallows-god, Hungry Tom, told them that "the velvet one" walks in a sleeping girl's skin. Provost Crane, Casimir's superior, showed him a red-and-gold thread found where Wick was hollowed. It came from Tilly's ribbon. Casimir lied to her about it. He secretly found Sump silt on Tilly's boots and said nothing.

Tilly was arrested and hooded, which cut Chat off. Chat began to thin, Thimble died, and only Greyhand stayed strong. Crane, who serves Vessary, the archpatron called the Velvet Eye, planned to have Tilly Veiled at the Lamplight Jubilee. Through her window, Vessary would end every small god in the city in a Final Tidying. Casimir broke Tilly out, and they hid with Rook in Mother Sluice's old canal-bed.

That night Casimir watched Tilly sleepwalk, milky-eyed, and start to hollow a sleeping man. Every hollowing had happened on a Curtain night. Hundreds of patrons left. Alone, Tilly nearly put out her own eye with a needle, but Pip stopped her. Together they worked out the truth: Greyhand is Vessary. By paying the largest share into her pact, it could ride her while she slept. Rook revealed that Vessary's warlocks set the fatal Marrowgate fire. He also explained how the Oculus lens could throw a warlock's sight onto every wall in the city. Tilly then told the plain truth of the Sump: her friend Dov died there and she left him behind. The story drew over fourteen hundred patrons. Casimir took the proof to Crane, and she found Vessary's jars of stolen sight.

At the Jubilee, the Lidless caught Tilly and bound her beneath the Eye. They cut her arms and broke the jars of sight over her. Chat gave everything, and Mother Sluice went out. The share stood level at fifty in the hundred. Then Casimir arrived with Crane, who was blinded as she gave orders. Through Tilly, Nine Bells spoke to him, his watch struck nine, and Nine Bells threw an archpatron's gold. The pact cast Greyhand out.
