Tilly is a young warlock with no single patron. Instead, hundreds of small, half-forgotten gods watch through her eyes at once, comment in a constant drift, and throw her power when she needs it. She calls them Chat. Among them are Mother Sluice, a canal goddess; Old Hob, a pedant; the heckler Gnash; gentle Thimble; and Greyhand, who never spoke and throws gold, the strongest power of all. Tilly made the pact in the Sump, a flooded quarry where small gods were dumped to starve, and claims she fell in alone. She lives at the Kettle Street Marionette Theater with Rook, an old showman.

Casimir is a Warden whose own patron has never spoken to him. People across the city were being "hollowed", left smiling and white-eyed, and he suspected Tilly. His superior, Provost Crane, lost her daughter when Marrowgate burned and wanted the many-patroned girl hooded. The Synod plans a Final Tidying of all small gods at the Lamplight Jubilee.

When Wick, a boy Tilly looked after, was hollowed, she made a deal with Casimir to hunt the hollower together. Old Hob recognised the hollowing as a draw that needs a window with many patrons. In the Sump, the feral god Hungry Tom told them that "the velvet one", a great god, walks in a sleeping girl's skin. Tilly's audience swelled past five hundred as she performed ever bigger stunts. A red-and-gold thread found at a crime scene matched Tilly's ribbon, and Casimir lied to Crane to protect her. Old Hob read from an old leaf of pact-law: a sleeping window is ridden by whoever holds the greatest share. Casimir found Sump silt on Tilly's boots and said nothing.

After a new victim named "the conjurer girl", Wardens hooded Tilly while Casimir stood by. For four days, blind and cut off, she kept Chat alive with stories. Thimble faded away, but Greyhand did not weaken. In a fever she glimpsed the Gallery, where the gods sit, and saw a grey glove among the small gods. Crane told Casimir that no one had been hollowed while Tilly was hooded. At the Jubilee, a great god called Vessary would use Tilly's severed window to destroy every small god.

Casimir broke her out. Chat had fallen to four hundred and two. They hid in vaults under the old Sixth Canal with Rook. Mother Sluice warned Tilly that Greyhand now holds nearly half her pact. Greyhand finally spoke: it wanted only her safety, and no one can break a pact from one side.

That night Casimir stayed awake. Tilly rose, her eyes milky, and walked to Cobb's Bridge, where she knelt over a sleeping man. When Casimir seized her, a voice that wasn't hers spoke his name. He dragged her into the canal. She came up awake to find her own thumbs on the man's eyelids, and one of his eyes had already turned white.
