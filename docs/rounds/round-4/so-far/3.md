Tilly is a young warlock in the city of Sallowcross with no single patron. Hundreds of lesser gods watch her at once and throw her power, and she calls them Chat. Among them are Gnash the heckler, Old Hob the historian, quiet Thimble, tiny Pip and motherly Mother Sluice. There is also Greyhand, who never speaks but throws gold when she is losing. She lives at the Kettle Street Marionette Theater with Rook, an old lantern-showman. Each night she says "Curtain" and the drift goes dark.

Casimir is a Warden hunting whoever is "hollowing" people, leaving them white-eyed and empty. His patron, the Abbess of Hours, has never spoken to him. His superior, Provost Crane, serves the god Vessary. The Synod plans a Final Tidying at the Lamplight Jubilee to wipe out the city's small gods. An earlier Tidying left them starving in a flooded quarry, the Sump.

When Wick, a boy from the theater, is hollowed, Tilly and Casimir join forces. A feral god in the Sump tells them a "velvet one" walks in a sleeping girl's skin. Casimir finds a thread from Tilly's ribbon on a victim and Sump silt on her boots, and he hides both. Even so, she is arrested and hooded, which cuts Chat off from her. Thimble fades away for good, but Greyhand never weakens.

Casimir breaks her out. One night he watches her sleepwalk, milky-eyed, and start hollowing a sleeping man, until he drags her into the water. Every hollowing fell on a night she said Curtain. Wick smiled because the last thing he saw was her face. When she tells Chat, many leave. She nearly puts out her own eye, but Pip stops her. From then on she sleeps with the house up.

Together they work it out. Greyhand is Vessary, the Velvet Eye. It has been riding her to harvest victims and frame her, and it plans to send the Final Tidying through her window into every small god. Rook reveals that at Marrowgate, Vessary's warlocks burned twenty-eight people. His plan is to use the Oculus's great lens with his lantern mirrors to throw Tilly's sight onto every wall in the city. If the city can see the small gods, no Tidying can starve them, and Tilly keeps the promise she made in the Sump.

Tilly goes live and tells the truth of the Sump: her friend Dov died there, and she climbed out and left him. The pact passes fourteen hundred and Greyhand's share drops. Casimir brings Crane the proof. She arrests him, then reads it all. She finds a vault of jars holding the hollowed and catches Vessary lying.

At the Jubilee, Tilly is carried inside a giant pageant puppet toward the Oculus. But the Lidless guards see with Vessary's eyes. They open the puppet and bind her at the focus beneath the Eye.
