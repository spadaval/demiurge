Merrit Foy is a warlock with no single patron. Instead, hundreds of lesser powers watch through her eyes at once, and she calls them Chat. On 4 April, auditor Ilse Brannock read Merrit's pact registration at the Exchange, where Merrit worked as a clerk. Ilse said she would recommend voiding the pact. Merrit answered that she would then be dead by Midsummer.

She had made the pact at midnight at Gallows Cross, with a stolen form and chalk lines that the archivist Dov copied from Wyck's old diagram. It was an open call to any power willing to share. Old Ember, a warm, dry voice, answered first, and then hundreds more. Termin, god of a vanished field boundary, set the terms: boons, and polls that bind her.

On her birthday her mother, Agathe, tried to bind her to the Undertow, as Agathe herself was bound. The Undertow found Merrit already taken. Agathe then told the price: when Merrit was a baby the Undertow saved her from a fever, and her life is owed back at the Midsummer tide.

Merrit refused the Registrar, Hesper Quayle, who wanted a Throne to sponsor the pact, and lost her clerkship. She grew her chorus from the dispossessed. A wraith killed gentle Saltlick. A generous patron, Benefice, arrived, and a silent one, Nobody-in-Particular, said only "Careful."

At the Spring Lists, losing to Corwin Sallow, whose patron, the Hound, holds him by a collar, Merrit let Benefice take the reins and won. To keep Chat watching, she paid toughs to ambush her and lied about it. Her flatmate Bet eventually left. Wyck's papers showed that a seat is held by watching, and that a fraud clause reaches back through every pact made under a fraudulent one. Hollow seats were found, all sold to Benefice. Merrit rescued Corwin after the Hound punished him.

Quayle had Merrit locked in the lightless Grey Room. Chat drained away while Benefice bought up the leaving seats. In the dark Merrit told them the truth, the ambush included, and they became a people. Ilse resigned to act as her advocate and freed her. The hearing is set for Midsummer Eve, the day before the tide.

Benefice then posted a binding poll that Merrit submit to the Undertow. In Quayle's office they found a ledger of seat sales made for the Undertow and a doctor's casebook describing frost on the cradle-rail and a fever without cause. Agathe arrived half-drowned, cut loose by the Undertow. She had been Nobody-in-Particular. Old Ember, once the family hearth, revealed that the Undertow caused the fever, then let go when Agathe bargained. That was fraud, and Ember will testify to it before the Court.

Termin expelled Benefice. The Undertow's revenge left a hundred and eighteen seats, and they held. Now Merrit walks the city of Cantle, telling forgotten things what a seat costs. Corwin walks with her, and the count has passed a thousand.
