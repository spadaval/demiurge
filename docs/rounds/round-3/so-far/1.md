On 4 April, in an interview room at the Exchange, the official Ilse Brannock reads a yellowed form XIV-c that nobody has filed in two hundred years. It registers a warlock who has hundreds of patrons instead of one, all watching at once. She calls them Chat. The warlock is Merrit, who works as a clerk at the Exchange herself. As she answers Ilse's questions, Chat comment inside her head. They adore Ilse, and one of them, Gnash, wants Merrit to bite her. Merrit registers the pact on the spot, which the Charter allows. Ilse says she will recommend voiding it. Merrit replies that if it is voided, she will be dead by Midsummer.

It began on 30 March. At midnight at Gallows Cross, with a stolen form and chalk lines her friend Dov had copied, Merrit called on any power willing to share. After a long silence a warm, dry voice, Old Ember, said "Well, there you are." Hundreds followed, among them Termin, who demanded to read the terms, and slow, fond Saltlick. At three in the morning she begged them to hush, Chat, and the name stuck.

The next morning Termin laid out the rules: a window, a quorum, boons and polls. He was once god of a boundary between two fields and is now a step in a tannery yard. Gnash called a poll that Merrit eat a pickled eel in Gutter Market. It carried, and she learned what it is to be compelled by a vote. Her friend Bet was furious that three hundred powers had watched Merrit wash, and so had seen Bet too.

At dawn on 2 April, her birthday, her mother Agathe took her to the half-flooded chapel at the river stairs. Agathe had been bound there, and Lisette before her. As Agathe spoke the words, the Undertow's cold broke against Merrit: she was already taken. Agathe, terrified, told Chat the price. Merrit had a fever at a week old, a life was given to save her, and that life is owed back at the Midsummer tide. That evening a silent patron, Nobody-in-Particular, took a seat.

On 5 April the Registrar, Hesper Quayle, offered to have a Throne sponsor the seats and protect Merrit. Merrit refused and was dismissed from her clerkship. Dov warned her that Quayle had taken a lamp down to the flooded sub-archive the night before.

Merrit began taking work. At a haunted bathhouse she offered the starving spirit a seat instead of burning it out, and it joined as Lady Calidarium. At the Hound's Tooth tavern she mocked the Exchange's golden boy, Corwin Sallow, with lines Chat fed her, and accepted his challenge to the Spring Lists. Alone afterward, he touched the gilt at his throat as if it hurt.

On 12 April a river-water wraith rose from the cellar drain. Saltlick died blocking its lunge at Merrit's heart, and twenty patrons left in fright. At Saltlick's wake a generous new patron, Benefice, gave her a boon that made her hands glow for an hour. Then Nobody-in-Particular spoke for the first time: "Careful."
